## Columns and formulas

Common manifest columns are `path`, `id`, `lang`, `repo`, `turns`, and the classification fields. One-shot `words` is `len(instruction.split())`; sequence classification cells preserve turn order. `verified` and `merged_at` remain UNKNOWN when a manifest omits them.

For one-shots, `run/pass/infra/leak` counts non-missing cells, PASS cells, INFRA cells, and LEAK cells. `pass_rate = PASS / (PASS + FAIL)`; INFRA and every LEAK variant are excluded. `failed` lists columns whose verdict contains FAIL. Total tokens are fresh input + output + reasoning + cache read. For each metric and model-client, the case value is divided by that column's median positive known value; `indexes` is the geometric mean of those ratios across columns. `effort = geomean(cost_index, minutes_index, tokens_index)`. A reported single step is UNKNOWN. Zero/UNKNOWN observations are omitted and counted in `excluded`. `medians` are the case's raw positive medians across model-client columns.

For sequences, `passes` preserves each column's passed/turns value and `pass_rate` is the arithmetic mean of the per-column turn pass rates. `total indexes` divide tokens and minutes by each model-client's median sequence and geometrically average the ratios. `per-turn indexes` repeat that calculation after dividing every sequence by its turn count. `effort = geomean(minutes_per_turn_index, tokens_per_turn_index)`, so sequence length alone does not make work look harder. The CSV also contains every per-column ratio and exclusion count.

Both tables are sorted by `pass_rate` ascending, then `effort` descending. Index 1.0 is typical for the historical model-client columns that ran the item.
